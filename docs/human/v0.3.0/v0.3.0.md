# Architectural review - LLM generated

This slice extends the product detail view with customer reviews and star ratings (`POST /api/products/:id/reviews` and `GET /api/products/:id/reviews`). Reviews are persisted in PostgreSQL with a foreign key to the product and survive catalogue re-imports.

Key architectural properties:
- **Cursor-based pagination:** Ordered newest-first (`createdAt DESC, id DESC`) with composite cursors to prevent pagination drift as new reviews arrive concurrently.
- **Dynamic aggregates:** Count and average score are computed per request over all matching reviews to ensure consistency without caching stale averages.
- **Idempotency guard:** Client-generated `submissionId` ensures network retries return existing reviews without creating duplicates, rejecting conflicting payloads with `409 Conflict`.
- **Degraded availability:** Product details and review submission remain accessible even if existing reviews fail to load, and reviews are preserved for phased-out or absent products.

Full architecture details are recorded in [architecture-proposal.md](../../agent/reviews/architecture-proposal.md) and [DECISIONS.md](../../../DECISIONS.md).
